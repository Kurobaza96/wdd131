About Guatemala

Guatemala is a beautiful country in Central America known for its mountains, volcanoes, lakes, forests, and rich cultural history. It is home to many Mayan communities and has a strong cultural connection to its indigenous heritage.

Guatemala has many interesting places to visit, including Antigua Guatemala, Lake Atitlán, Tikal, and Guatemala City. The country is also known for its colorful markets, traditional clothing, and delicious food.

Country Facts

  • Capital: Guatemala City
  • Continent: North America
  • Region: Central America
  • Currency: Guatemalan Quetzal
  • Official Language: Spanish
