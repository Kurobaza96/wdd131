Culture & Tradition

Food & Culture

Guatemalan cuisine reflects the country's history, geography, and cultural diversity.

Maya Influence

Maya culture has had a profound influence on Guatemalan cuisine. Corn, beans, chilies, tomatoes, and other native ingredients continue to play an important role in many traditional dishes.

Family Traditions

Many traditional recipes are prepared during family gatherings, holidays, celebrations, and religious events. Preparing food can become a way of preserving memories and passing knowledge between generations.

Regional Flavors

Guatemala's different regions have their own ingredients, cooking methods, and specialties. The result is a diverse culinary tradition where food can tell us about the community where it originated.
